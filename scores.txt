NASDECODE SCORING REFERENCE
===========================
Last methodology sync: 2026-10-04
Primary implementation:
- scripts/refresh.py: Nasdaq factor model, Semiconductor Leadership, Internal Confirmation.
- scripts/refine_scores.py: final Nasdaq publication layer, Consolidation Risk v3,
  Opportunity Score v3, SOXL Buy Score v2.

PURPOSE
-------
NasDecode deliberately keeps different questions in different scores:

1. Nasdaq Score (-100 to +100)
   Question: Is the current Nasdaq environment directionally supportive or restrictive?

2. Consolidation Risk (0 to 100)
   Question: How vulnerable is the current move to a 1-3 session fade, pause or sideways reset?

3. Opportunity Score (0 to 100)
   Question: How attractive is a new broad Nasdaq-oriented long entry now?

4. SOXL Buy Score (0 to 100)
   Question: How attractive is a new tactical SOXL long for roughly 1-5 sessions?

5. Internal Confirmation (0 to 100)
   Question: Are breadth, semiconductors, volatility and mega-caps confirming the tape?

6. Semiconductor Leadership (0 to 100)
   Question: Is semiconductor leadership broad and strong relative to Nasdaq/QQQ?

These scores are intentionally not interchangeable. A positive Nasdaq Score can coexist with
high Consolidation Risk or a poor SOXL entry because direction can be constructive while price
is too extended to chase.

COMMON RULES
------------
- Missing inputs are generally excluded rather than guessed.
- Where a weighted score excludes missing inputs, available weights are renormalized.
- 0-100 component scales are clipped to 0..100.
- Nasdaq directional factor strengths are clipped to -1..+1 before aggregation.
- Current-session data is preferred. Previous-close/stale inputs receive lower freshness weight
  in the Nasdaq directional model.
- Hard gates are applied after weighted SOXL/Opportunity calculations where documented below.
- A score is a decision aid, not an expected return, probability of profit or position-size rule.


======================================================================
1. NASDAQ SCORE
======================================================================

RANGE
-----
-100 to +100. Negative = restrictive/bearish environment. Positive = supportive/bullish
environment. Near zero = balanced/mixed.

HIGH-LEVEL FORMULA
------------------
Each dashboard factor receives:
  factor strength x base impact weight x freshness weight x regime multiplier.

Factors are grouped into correlation buckets so several versions of the same macro theme cannot
dominate merely because many related rows exist.

Buckets and maximum contribution weights:
- Rates / Macro:              cap 9
- Leadership:                 cap 9
- Breadth:                    cap 6
- Volatility / Positioning:   cap 7
- Event Risk:                 cap 4
- Other:                      cap 4

Raw Nasdaq Score =
  round(100 x SUM(bucket_strength x capped_bucket_weight)
            / SUM(capped_bucket_weight))

Base impact weights:
- Very High = 4
- High      = 3
- Medium    = 2
- Low       = 1

Freshness weights:
- Current/normal input = 1.00
- Previous close       = 0.70
- Stale/research snapshot/official-data lag = 0.35
- Awaiting/insufficient = 0.20
- Research-background factors are capped at 0.35 freshness weight.

Regime multipliers:
- Rates / Macro x1.25 when 10Y >= 4.85%
- Volatility / Positioning x1.20 when VXN >= 22
- Leadership x1.15 when that factor has High or Very High current impact

MAJOR CONTINUOUS FACTOR TRANSFORMS
----------------------------------
Treasury yields:
  level_strength    = clip((4.0 - yield_level) / 0.50, -1, +1)
  velocity_strength = clip(-daily_change_bp / 10, -1, +1)
  factor_strength   = 60% level + 40% velocity

Fed expectations:
  factor_strength = Fed Policy Score / 100, clipped -1..+1.

Semiconductor leadership inside Nasdaq Score:
  70% SOX relative strength + 15% SOXX relative strength + 15% SMH relative strength,
  with each relative-strength reading normalized over approximately +/-1.5 percentage points.

Mega-cap leadership:
  relative basket-vs-QQQ performance / 1.25pp, clipped -1..+1.

Nasdaq-100 breadth:
  (advancing_pct - 50) / 30, clipped -1..+1.

S&P 500 50D breadth:
  65% level strength + 35% change strength.
  level strength  = (percent above 50D - 50) / 25
  change strength = percentage-point change / 5
  both clipped -1..+1.

VXN/VIX:
  Uses both absolute volatility regimes and daily movement.
  VXN regime breakpoints: 15, 18, 23, 28, 33.
  VIX regime breakpoints: 12, 15, 20, 25, 30.
  factor = 50% VXN regime + 20% VIX regime
         + 20% inverse VXN daily move + 10% inverse VIX daily move.

Oil:
  factor_strength = negative average WTI/Brent daily percentage move / 3, clipped -1..+1.
  Falling oil is therefore supportive through the inflation/rates channel.

Options positioning:
  Uses Equity Put/Call level and its 5-day average:
  70% level signal + 30% trend signal.

Inflation · Labor · Growth:
  Uses the decaying verified macroImpulseScore on a -100..+100 Nasdaq-support scale.

Other factors:
  Fall back to positive = +1, mixed = 0, negative = -1.

CONFIDENCE
----------
Coverage = available base-weighted freshness / possible base weight.
Agreement = absolute average direction among active bucket strengths (|strength| >= 0.05).

Confidence =
  60% Coverage + 40% Agreement.

PUBLISHED NASDAQ SCORE
----------------------
scripts/refine_scores.py publishes effectiveScore when a bounded market-response layer is
available; otherwise it publishes rawScore. The result is rounded and clipped by the underlying
model design to the -100..+100 range.

GUIDANCE
--------
Score range       Label                  Operational interpretation
+45 to +100       Strong Positive        Favor long bias. Prefer entries with acceptable
                                         Opportunity/SOXL entry quality; do not chase solely
                                         because direction is strong.
+15 to +44        Cautiously Positive    Constructive but selective. Require confirmation from
                                         breadth/semiconductors and watch rates/volatility.
-14 to +14        Mixed / Neutral        No strong directional edge. Reduce conviction; favor
                                         patience, smaller exposure or event-driven setups.
-44 to -15        Cautiously Negative    Defensive bias. Avoid weak long setups; require clear
                                         recovery confirmation before adding risk.
-100 to -45       Strong Negative        Strongly restrictive environment. Avoid routine new
                                         longs unless a separate reversal framework confirms.

Important:
A high positive Nasdaq Score is NOT automatically a buy signal. Check Consolidation Risk and
Opportunity Score for entry timing.


======================================================================
2. CONSOLIDATION RISK v4
======================================================================

RANGE / PURPOSE
---------------
0 to 100. Higher = greater 1-3 session vulnerability to a fade, pause or sideways reset.
It is NOT a bearish-direction forecast and does not change the Nasdaq Score.

v4 separates two different ideas:
1. Structural extension: latent vulnerability created by price stretching away from trend.
2. Confirmation risk: evidence that the consolidation/fade is actually beginning.

This distinction is important near record/recent highs. Being at a high is not bearish by
itself. A high close to the 20DMA with normal RSI can be healthy trend behavior. A high reached
while QQQ/SOXX are far above their 20DMAs and overbought creates meaningful latent risk even
before breadth, leadership or intraday price action deteriorates.

A. STRUCTURAL EXTENSION
-----------------------
Structural Extension is a 0-100 weighted score:

- QQQ distance above 20DMA:          20%
- QQQ RSI14:                         15%
- QQQ 3D/5D acceleration:            10%
- QQQ high-proximity x extension:    15%
- SOXX distance above 20DMA:         15%
- SOXX RSI14:                        10%
- SOXX high-proximity x extension:   15%

Normalization:
- QQQ vs 20DMA:  +1% -> 0, +6% -> 100
- QQQ RSI14:       60 -> 0, 80 -> 100
- QQQ 3D return: +0.5% -> 0, +4% -> 100
- QQQ 5D return: +1.0% -> 0, +6% -> 100
  The available 3D/5D readings are averaged.
- SOXX vs 20DMA: +2% -> 0, +10% -> 100
- SOXX RSI14:      60 -> 0, 85 -> 100

High-proximity interaction:
- Distance from 20-day high = 0%  -> proximity 100
- 1% below 20-day high            -> proximity 50
- 2% or more below 20-day high    -> proximity 0

For each index:
  extension pressure = average(20DMA risk, RSI risk)
  high x extension   = extension pressure x high proximity / 100

Therefore an index merely being at a 20-day/record high does NOT automatically raise
Consolidation Risk. High proximity becomes important only when price is simultaneously
stretched above its 20DMA and/or RSI is elevated.

STRUCTURAL RISK FLOOR
---------------------
Structural Floor =
  65% x Structural Extension

This is the minimum latent Consolidation Risk before live deterioration is considered.
The 65% conversion intentionally prevents technical extension alone from implying that a
pullback has already started.

Example:
- Structural Extension 70 -> Structural Floor about 46.
Even with perfect intraday behavior, the market remains moderately vulnerable because price
location is stretched.

B. CONFIRMATION RISK
--------------------
Confirmation Risk is a separate 0-100 weighted score:

- Intraday rejection:           30%
- Breadth divergence:           25%
- Leadership divergence:        20%
- Volatility / rates stress:    20%
- Event risk:                    5%

Intraday rejection:
- Largest giveback from the best observed regular-session Nasdaq/QQQ return.
- 0.15pp giveback -> 0
- 1.25pp giveback -> 100

Breadth divergence:
- 55% Nasdaq breadth weakness
- 20% S&P 500 50D breadth weakness
- 10% deterioration in S&P 500 50D breadth
- +25 points when Nasdaq is >+0.35% but Nasdaq-100 breadth is <50%
- Result clipped 0..100.

Leadership divergence:
Average weakness of:
- SOX vs Nasdaq
- SOXX vs Nasdaq
- SMH vs Nasdaq
- Mega-cap basket vs QQQ
Semiconductor relative underperformance reaches maximum divergence around -1.0pp; mega-cap
underperformance around -0.8pp.

Volatility / rates stress:
Average normalized:
- VXN daily change: 0% -> 0, +6% -> 100
- 10Y daily change: 0bp -> 0, +8bp -> 100
- 2Y daily change:  0bp -> 0, +8bp -> 100
If Nasdaq is >+0.25% while both VXN and 10Y are rising, add 20 stress points, capped at 100.

Event risk:
- Pending high-read-through catalyst = 100
- Other pending catalyst = 25
- No pending catalyst = 0

FINAL FORMULA
-------------
Final Consolidation Risk =
  Structural Floor
  + (100 - Structural Floor) x Confirmation Risk / 100

Consequences:
- Strong trend confirmation can no longer erase meaningful technical extension.
- A stretched market can have Moderate risk even before rejection begins.
- Once rejection/divergence appears, the score rises from that structural baseline toward 100.
- A market near its highs but close to its 20DMA with normal RSI can still score Low.

DATA COVERAGE
-------------
coverageWeight reports the percentage of expected structural and confirmation inputs available.
historyDays reports the shorter stored-history length between QQQ and SOXX where available.
These fields are published with the score and displayed in Debug/UI rather than inferred.

GUIDANCE
--------
Score range       Label       Operational interpretation
0-34              Low         Trend location is relatively healthy and/or there is little
                              evidence of a near-term reset. Normal entry timing.
35-54             Moderate    Latent extension or early deterioration is meaningful. Avoid
                              chasing; prefer better price location or selective scaling.
55-74             Elevated    Extension plus confirmation risk is substantial. Prefer
                              pullbacks/reset and require renewed participation before adding.
75-100            High        Strong evidence of an unstable/overextended tape. Avoid chasing
                              strength; wait for reset or clear renewed confirmation.

Use with Nasdaq Score:
- Positive Nasdaq + Low Risk:
  Constructive direction with relatively healthy price location.
- Positive Nasdaq + Moderate/Elevated Risk:
  Bullish trend can continue, but entry timing is increasingly poor; "bullish but stretched."
- Positive Nasdaq + High Risk:
  Direction may still be positive, but fade/reset vulnerability is severe.
- Negative Nasdaq + High Risk:
  Weak direction plus confirmed instability; particularly defensive.

Use with Opportunity/SOXL:
Consolidation Risk describes the market's near-term fragility. Opportunity Score and SOXL Buy
Score remain the decision-oriented entry scores. A high Nasdaq Score does not override poor
price location, and strong semiconductor leadership does not erase SOXL/QQQ extension.

3. OPPORTUNITY SCORE v4
======================================================================

RANGE / PURPOSE
---------------
0 to 100. Measures the quality of deploying NEW general Nasdaq long exposure now.
It is an entry-quality score, not a forecast of the next session return.

Opportunity Score answers:
  "Given direction, participation, macro conditions, catalysts and CURRENT PRICE LOCATION,
   is this a good time to add long Nasdaq exposure?"

It deliberately distinguishes:
- A healthy/strong market from
- A good price at which to initiate a new position.

Strong internals can support continuation while price is too stretched to chase.

TOP-LEVEL WEIGHTS
-----------------
- Directional edge:        20%
- Entry quality:           35%
- Recovery confirmation:   20%
- Macro timing:            15%
- Catalyst asymmetry:      10%

A. DIRECTIONAL EDGE — 20%
-------------------------
  clip(Nasdaq Score x 1.25 + 50, 0, 100)

Examples:
  Nasdaq -40 -> 0
  Nasdaq   0 -> 50
  Nasdaq +20 -> 75
  Nasdaq +40 -> 100

This measures whether the broader Nasdaq setup is directionally supportive. It does not
determine whether the current price is attractive.

B. ENTRY QUALITY v4 — 35%
-------------------------
Entry Quality is now explicitly price-location aware.

Subweights:
- QQQ vs 20DMA:                  30%
- QQQ RSI14:                     20%
- QQQ 5D return:                 15%
- QQQ high x extension:          20%
- SOXX structural extension:     15%

QQQ vs 20DMA entry score:
  clip(75 - 12 x QQQ distance above 20DMA)
Examples:
  0% above 20DMA -> 75
  +3%            -> 39
  +5%            -> 15
Negative distance (price below 20DMA) improves this subscore, subject to the broader
direction/recovery gates.

QQQ RSI14 entry score:
  clip(75 - 2.5 x (RSI14 - 50))
Examples:
  RSI 50 -> 75
  RSI 60 -> 50
  RSI 70 -> 25
  RSI 80 -> 0

QQQ 5D-return entry score:
  clip(65 - 7 x QQQ 5D return %)
This discourages chasing a sharp five-session advance and improves after a controlled reset.

QQQ high x extension entry score:
  100 - QQQ high x extension risk from Consolidation Risk v4.

The underlying high interaction is:
  extension pressure = average(QQQ 20DMA risk, QQQ RSI risk)
  high x extension risk = extension pressure x 20-day-high proximity / 100

Therefore:
- QQQ at a 20-day/record high but close to its 20DMA with normal RSI is NOT heavily penalized.
- QQQ at the high while materially above its 20DMA and overbought receives a poor entry score.
- Pulling several percent below the high reduces this chase penalty.

SOXX structural-extension entry score:
  100 - average(
      SOXX 20DMA-extension risk,
      SOXX RSI risk,
      SOXX high x extension risk
  )

This matters because NasDecode is designed around a semiconductor-led Nasdaq. Strong SOXX
leadership is constructive for direction/recovery, but extreme SOXX price extension should
simultaneously reduce the attractiveness of CHASING new Nasdaq exposure.

VXN optionality bonus:
+10 to Entry Quality only when ALL are true:
- VXN is between 22 and 32 inclusive,
- overall Structural Extension is below 70,
- pre-bonus Entry Quality is at least 35.

This preserves the benefit of tradable volatility/dislocation without allowing volatility to
turn an already extreme chase into a good entry.

C. RECOVERY CONFIRMATION — 20%
------------------------------
Equal-weight average of:
- Internal Confirmation score
- Market breadth signal: positive 100 / mixed 50 / negative 0
- Semiconductor leadership signal: positive 100 / mixed 50 / negative 0
- Mega-cap leadership signal: positive 100 / mixed 50 / negative 0
- Nasdaq volatility signal: positive 100 / mixed 50 / negative 0

Recovery can be strong while Entry Quality is weak. That combination means:
  "The tape is healthy, but do not chase the current price."

D. MACRO TIMING — 15%
---------------------
Equal-weight average of:
- 10Y: clip(50 - 6 x daily bp change)
- 2Y:  clip(50 - 6 x daily bp change)
- Fed repricing: clip(50 + 0.5 x Fed Repricing Score)
- Oil signal: positive 100 / mixed 50 / negative 0

Current changes matter more than absolute levels for this tactical entry score.

E. CATALYST ASYMMETRY — 10%
---------------------------
Starts at 50.
For each relevant catalyst:
- Pending high-impact:        -15
- Other pending:               -5
- Observed positive:           +8
- Positive + persistent:      +15
- Observed negative:          -15
Final catalyst score is clipped 0..100.

RAW SCORE
---------
Raw Opportunity Score =
  20% Directional Edge
+ 35% Entry Quality
+ 20% Recovery Confirmation
+ 15% Macro Timing
+ 10% Catalyst Asymmetry

CONSOLIDATION / BEHAVIORAL GATES
--------------------------------
Consolidation Risk is NOT added as another weighted component because its 20DMA, RSI and
high-location inputs already overlap with Entry Quality. Instead it acts only as a final
sanity gate so strong recovery/catalyst readings cannot relabel a stretched chase as Attractive.

After the raw weighted calculation:

1. Weak tape gate
   Directional Edge <40 AND Recovery <50
   -> final Opportunity capped at 44.

2. Moderate consolidation-risk / poor-entry gate
   Consolidation Risk 35-54 AND Entry Quality <45
   -> final Opportunity capped at 59.

3. Elevated consolidation-risk / poor-entry gate
   Consolidation Risk 55-74 AND Entry Quality <50
   -> final Opportunity capped at 54.

4. High consolidation-risk gate
   Consolidation Risk >=75:
   - if Entry Quality <50 OR Recovery <70 -> cap at 44
   - otherwise -> cap at 49 pending stronger confirmation/reset.

5. Pending high-impact catalyst
   -> final Opportunity capped at 64.

The score stores both rawScore and final score, plus the active gates, so a capped result is
auditable.

WHY CONSOLIDATION RISK IS A GATE, NOT A SIXTH COMPONENT
--------------------------------------------------------
Opportunity v4 already sees price extension directly through Entry Quality. Subtracting or
weighting Consolidation Risk again would double-count QQQ/SOXX 20DMA, RSI and high proximity.

The gate has a narrower purpose:
  "Even if recovery and catalysts are excellent, should NasDecode be allowed to call this
   entry Attractive when price location is objectively poor?"

This produces coherent combinations such as:
- Strong recovery + stretched price -> Selective, wait for pullback.
- Strong recovery + healthy price location -> Attractive/Strong.
- Weak direction + cheap price -> still wait for confirmation.

GUIDANCE
--------
Score range       Label                    Operational interpretation
75-100            Strong Long Opportunity  Strong environment AND sufficiently good entry
                                           location for deploying new long capital.
60-74             Attractive               Favor adding exposure selectively. Price location
                                           and confirmation are supportive enough.
45-59             Selective                Constructive elements may exist, but price location,
                                           risk or confirmation argues against chasing. Prefer
                                           pullbacks, scaling or starter positions.
30-44             Wait for Confirmation    Conditions are not sufficiently aligned for routine
                                           new long exposure.
0-29              Poor Long Opportunity    Avoid routine new long entries; wait for material
                                           improvement in direction/recovery/price location.

OPPORTUNITY TYPE
----------------
- Recovery / dip entry:
  Recovery >=65 AND Entry Quality >=60 AND Directional Edge <60.
- Trend continuation:
  Directional Edge >=60 AND Recovery >=60 AND Entry Quality >=50.
- Wait for confirmation:
  Recovery <45.
- Otherwise:
  Selective / mixed.

RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NASDECODE SCORES
--------------------------------------
Nasdaq Score:
  Direction. Is the broader setup bullish, bearish or neutral?

Consolidation Risk:
  Fragility. Is the current move structurally stretched and/or beginning to deteriorate?

Opportunity Score:
  General Nasdaq entry decision. Is NOW a good price/time to add long exposure?

SOXL Buy Score:
  Instrument-specific leveraged-semiconductor entry decision. It can be much lower than
  Opportunity Score when SOXL itself is extremely extended.

A positive Nasdaq Score does not guarantee a high Opportunity Score. Likewise, strong
semiconductor leadership can raise Recovery Confirmation while simultaneously reducing Entry
Quality if SOXX has become technically overextended.


======================================================================

4. SOXL BUY SCORE v3.2
======================================================================

RANGE / HORIZON
---------------
0 to 100, tactical horizon approximately 1-5 sessions.
This is an entry-quality score for a DAILY 3x leveraged semiconductor ETF. It is not expected
return, probability of profit or a position-size recommendation.

MAJOR WEIGHTS — UNCHANGED FROM v2
---------------------------------
- Semiconductor trend / leadership: 30%
- SOXL entry quality:                25%
- Market confirmation:              15%
- Volatility / leverage risk:       15%
- Rates / macro:                    10%
- Catalyst asymmetry:                5%

v3 does NOT change these six weights. It changes how RSI and technical extension affect the
final score so price extension is continuous and regime-aware rather than a set of hard cliffs.

4.1 SEMICONDUCTOR TREND v2 (30%)
--------------------------------------
Semiconductor Trend is no longer an average containing the nested Semiconductor Leadership
score. That structure double-counted SOX/SOXX/SMH relative strength and made the trend overly
sensitive to one session.

New formula:
- SOXX Technical Trend:       30%
- SOX vs Nasdaq relative strength:  25%
- SOXX vs Nasdaq relative strength: 15%
- SMH vs Nasdaq relative strength:  10%
- Semiconductor Breadth:     20%

Relative-strength inputs are continuously scaled:
  -1.50pp vs Nasdaq = 0
   0.00pp           = 50
  +1.50pp           = 100
Values outside the range are clipped.

SOXX TECHNICAL TREND
--------------------
SOXX Technical Trend is itself:
- 30% SOXX distance from 20DMA
- 25% SOXX 5D return
- 25% SOXX RSI14 trend
- 20% SOXX directional ADX/DMI trend strength

SOXX vs 20DMA trend curve:
  <=-10% -> 0
    -5%  -> 25
     0%  -> 50
    +5%  -> 75
  >=+10% -> 100

SOXX 5D momentum curve:
  <=-5%  -> 0
  -2.5%  -> 25
     0%  -> 50
   +2.5% -> 75
  >=+5%  -> 100

SOXX RSI14 trend curve:
  <=35 -> 0
   40  -> 20
   45  -> 35
   50  -> 50
   55  -> 65
   60  -> 80
   65  -> 90
   70-80 -> 100
   >80 gradually eases toward 95 at RSI 100

Values between breakpoints are linearly interpolated.

SOXX ADX/DMI trend-strength curve:
- ADX <=15 -> neutral trend-strength score 50
- ADX 20 -> 65
- ADX 25 -> 80
- ADX 35 -> 95
- ADX >=45 -> 100
Values are linearly interpolated. ADX itself is directionless, so +DI/-DI supplies direction:
when +DI >= -DI the bullish score above is used; when -DI > +DI it is inverted (100-score).
Thus a high ADX strengthens an established uptrend but strongly penalizes an established
downtrend. Weak/no-trend ADX remains near neutral rather than pretending to know direction.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
---------------------
SOXX RSI is used here as a TREND-strength measure. RSI 70-80 therefore confirms a powerful
semiconductor trend. It does not make a new SOXL entry safer.

SOXL's own RSI is separately used inside Entry Quality and in the RSI x 20DMA overheating
penalty. Therefore the model can simultaneously say:
- Semiconductor Trend is very strong, AND
- SOXL itself is too overheated to chase.

Semiconductor Breadth is the confirmation layer's breadth score based on the share of tracked
semiconductor names outperforming QQQ.

This v2 construction intentionally gives 50% of Semiconductor Trend to persistent structure
(SOXX Technical Trend + Breadth) and 50% to current relative leadership. A single strong or weak
session can therefore change the score, but cannot by itself define the entire sector trend.


4.2 SOXL ENTRY QUALITY (25%)
----------------------------
Entry Quality factor weights remain:
- Distance from 20DMA: 30%
- 5D return:           20%
- RSI14:               20%
- 20D-high distance:   20%
- Intraday giveback:   10%

20DMA factor:
  clip(85 - 8 x positive distance_above_20DMA - 5 x distance_below_20DMA)

5D-return factor:
  clip(78 - 5 x max(5D_return - 4,0) - 4 x max(-5D_return - 3,0))

RSI14 factor — v3 momentum-aware curve:
  RSI 40 -> 40
  RSI 45 -> 55
  RSI 50 -> 70
  RSI 55 -> 85
  RSI 60 -> 100
  RSI 65 -> 95
  RSI 70 -> 80
  RSI 75 -> 60
  RSI 80 -> 35
  RSI 85 -> 10
  RSI 90+ -> 0
Values between breakpoints are linearly interpolated.

Purpose:
RSI 55-65 is treated as healthy momentum for a leveraged semiconductor trend rather than as
overbought. Above 75, overheating increasingly reduces entry quality; above 80 the penalty
accelerates.

20-day-high factor with strong SOX:
A controlled pullback from the recent high is rewarded using:
  0% below high -> 25
  2% -> 40
  4% -> 55
  6% -> 70
  8% -> 82
  10% -> 90
  12% -> 95
  15% -> 75
  20% -> 35
  25% -> 10
The reward peaks near a 12% pullback and then falls because a deeper drawdown can indicate
breakdown rather than a bargain.

Without strong SOX:
  score = clip(82 - 5 x abs(distanceFrom20dHighPct + 4))

Intraday giveback:
  score = clip(100 - 25 x giveback_percentage_points)
The factor is excluded when no current-session SOXL observation exists.

4.3 MARKET CONFIRMATION (15%)
-----------------------------
Equal-weight average of:
- Internal Confirmation
- Nasdaq directional edge = clip(Nasdaq Score x 1.25 + 50)
- Market breadth signal = 100 / 50 / 0
- Mega-cap leadership signal = 100 / 50 / 0

4.4 VOLATILITY / LEVERAGE RISK (15%)
-------------------------------------
Weighted safety score:
- 35% 100 - Consolidation Risk
- 25% Nasdaq volatility / VXN condition
- 15% VXN daily-change safety
- 25% SOXL ATR regime safety

SOXL ATR REGIME
---------------
NasDecode calculates Wilder ATR(14) directly from stored SOXL OHLC bars.

Published context:
- ATR14 as % of SOXL price
- ATR14% percentile within the most recent 60 available ATR observations
- ATR14 / ATR50 ratio

ATR% is descriptive; the score is relative to SOXL's own volatility history rather than using
a fixed absolute ATR threshold for a naturally volatile 3x ETF.

ATR percentile safety = 100 - ATR14% 60-session percentile.

ATR expansion safety:
- ATR14/ATR50 <=0.80 -> 100
- 1.00 -> 80
- 1.20 -> 55
- 1.50 -> 25
- >=1.80 -> 0
Interpolated continuously.

SOXL ATR regime safety = average(ATR percentile safety, ATR expansion safety).

This means high but contracting/normal-for-SOXL volatility is less punitive than a fresh
realized-volatility expansion. ATR does not predict direction; it only changes leverage risk.

4.5 RATES / MACRO (10%)
-----------------------
Equal-weight average of available:
- clip(50 - 6 x 10Y daily bp change)
- clip(50 - 6 x 2Y daily bp change)
- clip(50 + 0.5 x Fed Repricing Score)
- Inflation · Labor · Growth signal = 100 / 50 / 0
- Oil signal = 100 / 50 / 0

4.6 CATALYST ASYMMETRY (5%)
---------------------------
Uses the same catalyst framework as Opportunity Score.

RAW SCORE
---------
Raw SOXL Score =
  30% Semiconductor Trend
+ 25% SOXL Entry Quality
+ 15% Market Confirmation
+ 15% Volatility / Leverage Risk
+ 10% Rates / Macro
+  5% Catalyst Asymmetry

v3 then applies two CONTINUOUS technical penalties before genuine risk gates.

A. CONTINUOUS 20DMA EXTENSION PENALTY
-------------------------------------
Base penalty:
- <=8% above 20DMA: 0
- 12% above:         4
- 18% above:        12
- 24% above:        24
- 30%+ above:       35
Values between points are linearly interpolated.

SEMICONDUCTOR-REGIME RELIEF
---------------------------
Strong sector confirmation may reduce, but never reverse, the 20DMA extension penalty:

- Semiconductor Trend >=85 AND Market Confirmation >=60:
    applied penalty = base penalty x 0.65
- Otherwise Semiconductor Trend >=75:
    applied penalty = base penalty x 0.80
- Otherwise:
    applied penalty = base penalty x 1.00

This means +18% above the 20DMA is still a negative entry-location input, but it is less
punitive when SOX/SOXX/SMH and the broader tape strongly confirm the move.

B. RSI x 20DMA EXTREME-OVERHEATING PENALTY
-------------------------------------------
RSI heat penalty:
- RSI <=75: 0
- RSI 80:  10
- RSI 85:  20
- RSI 90+: 30
Interpolated continuously.

20DMA amplifier:
- <=8% above: 0.25
- 15% above:  0.50
- 20% above:  0.80
- 25%+ above: 1.00
Interpolated continuously.

Extreme overheating penalty =
  RSI heat penalty x 20DMA amplifier

This penalty does NOT receive the full semiconductor-regime relief. Strong SOX can justify
persistent trend extension, but it does not make RSI in the mid/high-80s harmless for a daily
3x ETF.

PRE-GATE SCORE
--------------
Pre-gate SOXL Score =
  Raw SOXL Score
- Regime-adjusted 20DMA extension penalty
- RSI x 20DMA extreme-overheating penalty

The result is floored at zero.

HARD GATES — ONLY GENUINE REGIME / EVENT RISKS
-----------------------------------------------
The old SOXL >8%, >12%, >18% 20DMA hard caps are REMOVED.
The old >15% above 20DMA + RSI>80 cap-at-25 rule is REMOVED.

Remaining hard gates:
- Semiconductor Trend <40 -> cap final score at 45.
- At least two semiconductor relative-strength readings available and all negative -> cap 50.
- Consolidation Risk >=75 -> cap 45.
- VXN daily change >+8% AND 10Y >+5bp -> cap 45.
- Pending high-impact semiconductor catalyst -> cap 64.

WHY v3
------
v2 correctly warned against chasing SOXL, but a single 20DMA threshold could discard otherwise
valuable information about a persistent semiconductor momentum regime. v3 keeps extension
expensive while distinguishing:
- strong but extended,
- strong + extended + genuinely overheated,
- and extended without semiconductor confirmation.

The score publishes rawScore, preGateScore, the RSI factor score, the base/applied 20DMA
penalty, regime multiplier, RSI heat, RSI/20DMA interaction penalty and final gates.

GUIDANCE
--------
Score range       Label                       Operational interpretation
85-100            Exceptional Tactical Entry  Rare alignment of trend, price location and risk.
70-84             Attractive                  Good tactical long setup; normal risk controls.
55-69             Selective / Starter         Constructive setup. Prefer starter/scaled entry.
40-54             Wait                        Some edge exists but entry/risk is insufficient.
25-39             Poor Entry                  Do not chase; timing/extension remains unfavorable.
0-24              Avoid Long                  Tactical long setup is materially unfavorable.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION
------------------------
Strong Semiconductor Trend + RSI 55-65:
  Healthy momentum. RSI itself should support Entry Quality even if 20DMA distance still
  imposes an extension penalty.

Strong Semiconductor Trend + SOXL far above 20DMA + RSI <75:
  Strong but extended. Score should usually remain Selective/Wait rather than collapse solely
  because a hard 20DMA threshold was crossed.

SOXL far above 20DMA + RSI >80:
  Strong and genuinely overheated. Both the normal RSI Entry Quality factor and the separate
  RSI x 20DMA penalty reduce the score.

Weak Semiconductor Trend:
  A technically cheaper SOXL is not automatically a good entry; weak trend remains a hard
  regime warning.

======================================================================
6. SEMICONDUCTOR LEADERSHIP
======================================================================

FORMULA
-------
Semiconductor Leadership =
  55% SOX relative-strength score
+ 15% SOXX relative-strength score
+ 10% SMH relative-strength score
+ 20% Semiconductor Breadth score

Relative-strength score mapping used by the confirmation layer:
- >= +0.50pp -> 100
- >= +0.20pp -> 75
- >  -0.20pp -> 50
- >  -0.50pp -> 25
- <= -0.50pp -> 0

Semiconductor breadth:
Share of available tracked semiconductor names outperforming QQQ.
- >=70% -> 100
- >=55% -> 75
- >=45% -> 50
- >=30% -> 25
- <30%   -> 0

Broad leadership trigger:
If SOX >= +0.50pp vs Nasdaq AND SOXX >0pp vs Nasdaq AND SMH >0pp vs Nasdaq,
Semiconductor Leadership is floored at 75.

Labels:
75-100  Strong
60-74   Positive
40-59   Mixed
25-39   Weak
0-24    Negative

GUIDANCE
--------
75-100: Strong broad chip leadership; supportive for Nasdaq and SOXL trend.
60-74:  Positive leadership; useful but not full broad confirmation.
40-59:  Mixed; do not assume semiconductor-led Nasdaq strength.
25-39:  Weak leadership; SOXL tactical longs need unusually good entry quality.
0-24:   Negative leadership; semiconductor weakness is a major warning.

HOW TO USE THE SCORE SET TOGETHER
======================================================================

A useful decision sequence is:

1. Nasdaq Score: direction
   "Do I want a bullish, neutral or defensive market bias?"

2. Internal Confirmation / Semiconductor Leadership: confirmation
   "Is the tape actually confirming that directional view?"

3. Consolidation Risk: fragility
   "Even if direction is right, is the current move vulnerable to a near-term reset?"

4. Opportunity Score: broad entry timing
   "Is this a good moment to deploy new long capital?"

5. SOXL Buy Score: instrument-specific timing
   "Given 3x leverage and semiconductor conditions, is SOXL itself a good tactical entry?"

Example:
- Nasdaq Score +50
- Semiconductor Leadership 90
- Consolidation Risk 65
- Opportunity Score 58
- SOXL Buy Score 30

Interpretation:
The directional and semiconductor trend are strong, but the move is extended and SOXL entry
quality is poor. The model says "bullish environment, bad chase" rather than "buy because chips
are strong."
