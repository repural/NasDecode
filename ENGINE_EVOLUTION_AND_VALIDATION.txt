NasDecode Engine Evolution and Validation
=========================================

Status date: 2026-10-06
Scope: repural/NasDecode only. NasCal is not part of this work.

PURPOSE
-------
NasDecode separates four concepts that should not be collapsed into one score:
1. Resilience: whether semiconductor leadership survives adverse macro conditions.
2. Directional setup: what market state SOX is in and what historically followed that state.
3. Hype Pressure: exogenous narrative/event support, kept separate until enough prospective history exists.
4. SOXL Entry Quality: trade-entry/timing conditions for the leveraged ETF, separate from direction.

RESILIENCE ENGINE EVOLUTION
---------------------------
Phase 1 created a rolling one-year, point-in-time input history for SOX, QQQ, semiconductor ETFs/basket and macro variables. Hype history was joined point-in-time but not allowed to affect production scores.

Phase 2 introduced a walk-forward SOX expected-return model. The core object became the residual: actual SOX performance minus performance expected from market/macro conditions.

Phase 3 created the Resilience Engine. The score combines multi-horizon residual strength, persistence of positive residuals, and semiconductor internal confirmation. Macro sensitivities are diagnostics rather than direct score weights. Resilience remains shadow/validation only.

A forward-return backtest showed that Resilience is not a clean short-horizon directional predictor. Pearson relationships to future SOX were approximately -0.045/-0.063/-0.067/+0.150 at T+1/T+3/T+5/T+10. The correct interpretation is structural/regime durability, not “higher Resilience means buy tomorrow.”

DIRECTIONAL EDGE v1.0
---------------------
v1.0 attempted a monotonic directional score:
- 45% multi-horizon absolute SOX momentum
- 35% SOX-vs-QQQ relative momentum
- 20% semiconductor breadth persistence

Retrospective validation failed. SOX correlations at T+1/T+3/T+5/T+10 were -0.069/-0.115/-0.142/-0.025. Low scores sometimes preceded the strongest gains, revealing a non-monotonic/U-shaped relationship: both continuation and reversal can generate positive forward returns.

DIRECTIONAL EDGE v1.1
---------------------
v1.1 mapped raw strength into a U-shaped “setup quality” representation. Retrospective correlations improved to roughly +0.018/+0.098/+0.080/+0.128 for SOX.

This result was NOT accepted as proof because the U-shaped mapping was designed after observing the same historical sample. It is explicitly treated as in-sample and susceptible to overfitting/look-ahead research bias.

STRICT WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION
------------------------------
A strict expanding-window test was then built. For each date T, only earlier observations whose T+5 outcome had already matured were eligible for training. The hand-tuned v1.1 U-curve was excluded.

Signal span: 2026-01-23 through 2026-10-05, 176 signals.
SOX correlations T+1/T+3/T+5/T+10:
-0.0226 / -0.0063 / -0.0172 / -0.0964.
SOXL:
-0.0332 / -0.0340 / -0.0467 / -0.1134.

Conclusion: the attractive v1.1 retrospective relationship did not survive strict chronological learning. Directional Edge was not promoted.

DIRECTIONAL REGIMES v2
----------------------
v2 separated Continuation and Reversal rather than forcing them onto one continuum.

Continuation used established SOX trend, relative leadership, participation and recent confirmation. Reversal used prior weakness plus turn evidence, with an explicit gate so weakness alone could not score highly.

Retrospective results again failed:
Continuation SOX correlations: -0.075/-0.106/-0.132/-0.028.
Reversal SOX correlations: +0.010/+0.023/-0.007/-0.054.
Continuation high-score observations did not outperform reliably, while reversal high-score sample sizes were too small.

No historical threshold tuning was performed after this failure.

DIRECTIONAL ENGINE v3 RESEARCH DESIGN
-------------------------------------
v3 changed the hypothesis instead of adjusting v2 thresholds. The model became an explicit state-transition classifier.

Development period ends 2026-06-30.
A July 1, 2026 onward holdout was locked for the v3 implementation.

Important caveat: this is a pseudo-out-of-sample research holdout, not pristine OOS data. Earlier v1/v2 research had already exposed the overall research process to the one-year period. Within the v3 process, however, holdout outcomes were not inspected while defining/finalizing v3. True prospective shadow validation remains the strongest test.

Development-only states and results:
- Trend persistence: n=47; SOX mean +0.37/+1.46/+1.76/+5.05% at T+1/T+3/T+5/T+10.
- Uptrend pullback: n=20 (19 at longer horizons); +0.32/+1.38/+3.20/+3.30%; T+5 positive 78.9%.
- Washout unconfirmed: n=19; +0.73/+3.00/+3.84/+7.83%; T+5 positive 78.9%.
- Confirmed recovery: n=10; -0.37/-0.08/+0.66/+3.21%.

The development evidence indicated that requiring rebound confirmation made the engine late. This explained part of the earlier U-shape: established trends and washed-out markets can both lead to positive returns for different reasons.

FROZEN DIRECTIONAL ENGINE v3.0
------------------------------
The candidate was frozen in Git commit 491442dd400ab34f48e8cbed3515b6db7a55cd68 before holdout inspection.
Frozen specification SHA-256:
cf7ede6ff6312720f2b31d0c9a557c484039bb533fa7e04c3a078cc5583157c3

States:
- Uptrend Pullback: SOX 10D >3%, 10D SOX-vs-QQQ relative strength >1pp, current SOX day negative but >-3%.
- Washout/Rebound: SOX 5D <-2% and SOX 10D <0%. No same-day recovery confirmation is required.
- Continuation: SOX 5D >2%, SOX 10D >3%, 5D relative strength >0.5pp, semiconductor basket positive breadth >=60%, current SOX day >=0.
- Neutral/Other: everything else.

This is a setup classifier, not a monotonic 0-100 directional score. SOXL-specific indicators are not classifier inputs.

SOXL VALIDATION DATA ISSUE AND REMEDIATION
------------------------------------------
The first v3 holdout run produced n=0 for SOXL because SOXL was not included in the canonical one-year resilience history. This was a data-allocation omission, not a model result.

Commit 8480ed5184d5903570f91f80b5c66778be38ecc4 adds SOXL to the canonical Massive daily history. The exact frozen v3 classifier must be rerun against SOXL as an OUTCOME ONLY. No v3 thresholds or classifications may be changed as a result of adding SOXL history.

ANTI-OVERFIT / VALIDATION RULES
-------------------------------
- Never use future data in a signal at T.
- Forward outcomes are evaluation targets, never classifier inputs.
- Do not tune v3.0 after opening its holdout. Any redesign requires a new engine version.
- Report sample count, mean, median and hit rate; means alone can be dominated by outliers.
- Prefer chronological/walk-forward validation to random train/test splits for market data.
- Overlapping T+3/T+5/T+10 returns reduce statistical independence and must be acknowledged.
- SOXL is leveraged and path-dependent; it is an outcome/timing instrument, not a substitute for SOX direction.
- Hype Pressure remains separate until sufficient prospective point-in-time history exists.
- Prospective frozen shadow performance is the final standard before production promotion.

CURRENT STATUS
--------------
Resilience Engine: shadow structural/regime measure; not a proven short-term directional predictor.
Hype Engine: shadow, point-in-time collection; insufficient prospective history for production weighting.
Directional v3.0 Washout/Rebound: validated candidate in pseudo-OOS holdout; prospective validation required.
Directional v3.0 Uptrend Pullback: promising, insufficient holdout sample.
Directional v3.0 Continuation: failed as forward entry signal; regime descriptor only.
SOXL Entry Quality: separate timing layer; must not be used to manufacture directional validation.

No NasCal files or workflows were modified as part of this work.
